Built for the 2011 Capstone Design Contest, Tachikoma was a large, heavy quadruped robot combining walking legs with wheels, requiring substantial electrical power. While the hybrid leg–wheel mechanism had limited practical effect, it remains one of SIGMA's few large-scale legged robots.
