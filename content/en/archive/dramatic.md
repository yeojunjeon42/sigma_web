An RFID-based smart wardrobe and styling recommendation system, planned as an entry for the Samsung Electronics Home Appliance Concept Contest.

- Provides garment care and washing instructions via NFC tags
- Locates specific garments inside an RFID-equipped closet via a mobile app
- Recommends daily outfits synced with live weather forecasts
