Created for the 2011 Creative Engineering Design Contest, iDEN is one of SIGMA's flagship builds. Designed as a smooth, spherical home robot platform with no sharp edges for household safety, iDEN supports swappable modular attachments for diverse domestic tasks.

iDEN won 1st place at the on-campus Capstone Design Contest, received the Minister of Education Award at the National Capstone Design Contest, and won 1st place at the International Capstone Design Contest held at Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), China, on January 11–13, 2012, where it was recognized for presenting a new concept for robot platforms. Now displayed in the club room, iDEN inspired many later SIGMA builds.
