## Overview

Scout is a robot capable of driving on both floors and vertical walls. Moving beyond standard four-wheeled robots limited to flat surfaces, the project inverted the hovercraft principle—using a propeller to create suction rather than lift—to achieve agile mobility and energy efficiency across vertical surfaces.

## How it works

Scout houses a centrally mounted motor oriented perpendicular to the driving surface. This vertical BLDC motor, spinning at over 3,000 RPM, drives a propeller that creates a low-pressure zone between the robot and the wall, increasing normal force and friction at the drive wheels so the robot adheres to the wall. Two motorized wheels then propel the robot across the vertical surface.
