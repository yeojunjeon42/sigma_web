Full Count is a low-cost baseball pitch tracker built for amateur players using an array of ultrasonic sensors. By arranging multiple ultrasonic transducers to avoid cross-talk and applying signal filtering, it tracks pitch trajectories and displays them through a user-friendly interface. Full Count won 2nd place at the SNU Creative Design Fair and 3rd place at the ECE Electronics Fair.
