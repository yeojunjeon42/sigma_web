## Overview

Inspired by "The Most Useless Machine Ever," this project focuses on playful human–robot interaction rather than practical utility, creating a small robot that feels expressive and alive.

## How it works

When the user flips a toggle switch on, the robot reaches out to turn the switch back off. Using an infrared sensor, it probabilistically dodges an approaching hand and displays reactive facial expressions on a front LED dot-matrix module. Hardware control runs on an Arduino Uno driving the dot-matrix display, IR sensor, two servo motors, and two DC motors with a motor driver module.
