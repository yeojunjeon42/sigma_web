AngelSwing began as a student nonprofit project to supply low-cost mapping drones to disaster-affected areas following the 2015 Nepal earthquake. Raising 3,000,000 KRW via crowdfunding, the team built and tested custom UAVs.

From August 3 to 10, 2015, the team traveled to earthquake-damaged regions in Nepal, conducted aerial surveys, and delivered orthomosaic imagery and 3D terrain models to Kathmandu University, village leaders, and relief agencies to aid reconstruction. They also hosted a four-day open-source UAV workshop at Kathmandu University to share appropriate-technology drone building methods. AngelSwing later grew into a social enterprise.
