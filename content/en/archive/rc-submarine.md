## Overview

Build an RC submarine and launch it in Jahayeon Pond on the SNU campus.

## Process

Divided tasks so components that could be built in parallel were worked on simultaneously.

Organized components according to assembly sub-stages.

## Results

Check connections and component operation frequently; soldering problems alone took three to four weeks to resolve.

Use solder paste for fine-pitch SMD soldering on PCBs.

Dividing roles clearly works well.
