ISAC (Intelligent Self-Assembly Colonies) is a modular self-assembling swarm exploration robot designed for hazardous scientific exploration and disaster search-and-rescue missions.

ISAC modules can split into independent units to map large areas in parallel and autonomously dock back together when larger traction or capabilities are needed. If an individual unit is damaged in rough terrain, the colony can detach the damaged module and continue operating without mission failure. This architecture earned 3rd place in the 2014 Samsung Techwin Robot Membership, Silver at the ECE Electronics Fair, and KOFAC Outstanding Research recognition.
