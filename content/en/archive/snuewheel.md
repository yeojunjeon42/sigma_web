## Overview

A motorized front bicycle wheel with built-in electronics that converts a standard bicycle into an electric bicycle.

1) Develop an electric bicycle conversion wheel that anyone can install easily
2) Design a speed- and attitude-based pedal assist system (PAS)
3) Design an internal gear power-transmission system

## How it works

Commercial e-bike conversion kits often require complex mechanical installation. SNUEWheel allows anyone with no mechanical background to convert a standard bicycle into an e-bike in under one minute simply by swapping the front wheel.

First, instead of relying on expensive bottom-bracket torque sensors, SNUEWheel integrates an infrared speed sensor, accelerometer, and gyroscope into a single self-contained hub unit, using an open-loop physical resistance model based on bicycle speed and slope to deliver smooth pedal assistance.

Second, the internal batteries and electronics remain stationary inside the hub while only the outer rim rotates, minimizing rotational inertia and improving riding efficiency.
