A modular LED cube display built to challenge the convention that screens must be flat rectangles, allowing individual cube modules to be assembled into arbitrary 3D display shapes.

Unlike most club builds at the time, the acrylic machining was outsourced and the electronics used custom PCBs. The modular design was practical enough that filing a patent was discussed after the competition.
