POCLAINGER is a wearable arm exoskeleton designed for intuitive control of heavy excavation machinery. By mapping natural operator arm movements directly to excavator joints, it simplifies complex heavy-equipment operation. The project won 1st place at the on-campus Creative Design Contest, Gold at the ECE Electronics Fair, and 1st place in the ROBOTIS OpenCM Challenge.
