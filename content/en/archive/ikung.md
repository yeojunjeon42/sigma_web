iKung is an inflatable wearable suit that helps children with visual impairments run and play safely indoors. The air-filled spherical suit cushions collisions while front-mounted ultrasonic sensors emit proximity audio cues and transmit collision data to a server to enable interactive games such as tag. iKung won 1st place at the SNU Creative Design Fair and Gold at the ECE Electronics Fair.
