A quadruped walking robot built for the 2012 Creative Engineering Design Contest using an embedded Linux board. Just days before the competition, all of its motors and control boards burned out, preventing a live demonstration. Nevertheless, the project marked a turning point toward adopting formal control algorithms and structured mechanical design in subsequent club builds.
