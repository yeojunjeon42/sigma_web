## Overview

Quri is a conversational AI robot with its own language. Created for the 2017 Spark Road overseas travel competition, the project started from the idea of building a robot travel companion. Quri aims to foster emotional connection and mutual understanding with people—even though its language and expressions differ from human ones, it actively seeks to communicate and stay by the user's side.

## How it works

We focused on designing Quri's language and facial expressions so that humans can intuitively understand Quri's emotions and thoughts despite its non-human mode of expression.

Quri's spoken language is built around the syllable "Q," varying pitch and repetition to express joy, sadness, attention-seeking, boredom, and curiosity. Rather than conveying detailed sentences directly, this vocal language acts as a bridge to deeper communication. When a user brings an Android phone close to Quri's body, NFC and Bluetooth auto-pairing launch a Google Instant App—requiring no installation—that acts as a Quri translator displaying detailed messages.

Quri runs on a Raspberry Pi 3, with an Arduino controlling LED strips for facial expressions and a top-mounted camera performing computer vision to detect human presence and location.
