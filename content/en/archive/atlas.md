Prior to iDEN, ATLAS was the largest-scale robot built by the club, costing over 10,000,000 KRW. Designed as a search-and-rescue robot, it housed an entire desktop PC inside a hand-fabricated chassis with caterpillar tracks capable of climbing stairs, along with early computer vision features. ATLAS won Gold (1st place) at the ECE Electronics Fair.
