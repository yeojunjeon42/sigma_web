## Overview

Locked Charger is an anti-theft charging station designed to prevent smartphones from being stolen or unplugged while charging in public places such as cafes.

## How it works

An Arduino monitors charging current through a current sensor. If a connected device is unplugged while the alarm is armed, the sudden drop in current triggers a loud buzzer alarm until normal current draw is restored. A password keypad lets the owner arm and disarm the alarm when unplugging their phone.
