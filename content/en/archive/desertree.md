## Overview

In Inner Mongolia's Mu Us Desert, one person—Yin Yuzhen—planted trees over 20 years to turn barren sand into a 46-square-kilometer forest. Inspired by this effort, Desertree is an automated tree-planting robot designed to accelerate reforestation in desertifying regions.

## How it works

Modeled after mechanical rice transplanters, Desertree plants tree seedlings in fine desert sand by displacing sand rather than scooping it up. Planting occurs in three steps: first, four nested 3D-printed spades plunge vertically into the sand; second, the spades spread horizontally to open a pocket for the seedling; third, the spades retract vertically, allowing the displaced sand to collapse back around the roots. The chassis is built from acrylic and controlled by an Arduino driving the planting actuators.
