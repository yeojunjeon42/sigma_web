Inspired by the open-source OpenROV movement and the growing consumer drone market, ROVision aimed to build an affordable remotely operated underwater vehicle (ROV) paired with augmented reality and web-based interfaces for marine exploration and entertainment.

The electronics pair a BeagleBone Black—which hosts an onboard web server for topside communication—with an ARM microcontroller that reads sensors and controls thrusters. To avoid expensive neutral-buoyancy multi-core tether cables, ROVision uses power-line communication (PLC) to transmit control and video signals over a thin two-wire tether, while BLDC thrusters driven by drone ESCs provide underwater maneuverability.
