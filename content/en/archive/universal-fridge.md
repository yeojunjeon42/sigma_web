## Overview

An ideal kitchen is one where everyone can cook comfortably. However, for people with visual impairments, simply finding and retrieving ingredients from a refrigerator—and checking food freshness using only smell and touch—is challenging. Sighted people also frequently struggle with forgotten items expiring in the back of the fridge. To solve this, we designed "FriTips," a universal refrigerator storage system.

With FriTips, users never have to search blindly for ingredients. Users place a food container in any slot and record a voice memo describing its contents. Later, selecting a slot intuitively plays back what is stored there. Moving a container to another slot does not require re-recording, and emptying a container lets the user delete the entry easily. FriTips also tracks expiration dates entered during storage and alerts users on the day an item expires.

## Motivation

Preparing a meal engages all the senses and brings a strong sense of accomplishment. According to the Korea Foundation for the Welfare of the Visually Impaired, many people with visual impairments want to learn to cook independently. Currently, they often organize refrigerators by grouping similar items into standardized labeled containers, yet locating specific ingredients and tracking expiration dates remains difficult. We built FriTips to solve these everyday storage challenges.

## Process

Our goal was to build a universal refrigerator organizer that helps both people with visual impairments and general users track food locations and expiration dates. To detect container placement electronically without requiring custom-built containers, we designed detachable elastic clips that mount onto existing household containers. To fit various container sizes, we 3D-printed three sizes of adjustable slider attachments covering 8 cm–13 cm, 13 cm–20 cm, and 18 cm–27 cm, mounted on sliding rails so containers can be arranged efficiently on the base unit.

## Impact

Because the base unit is modular and easy to install, FriTips offers broad compatibility. Built around an affordable Arduino embedded system, it has a low barrier to entry and strong commercial potential. Beyond refrigerators, the same location- and inventory-tracking architecture can be extended to pantries, closets, and IoT home organization systems so anyone can manage household items equally and conveniently.
