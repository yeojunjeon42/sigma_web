A scale autonomous model car equipped with a self-driving system.

Built around an Infineon TC-237 MCU, infrared sensors for obstacle detection, and DC motors with encoders for drive control. We developed obstacle-avoidance and path-estimation algorithms enabling autonomous navigation along user-defined routes, with GPS waypoint navigation planned as a follow-up feature.
