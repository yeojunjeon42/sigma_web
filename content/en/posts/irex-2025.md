From December 2 to 7, 2025, SIGMA's Cotutor team conducted field research in Tokyo, Japan, and attended the International Robot Exhibition (iREX) 2025 held during the same period.

Cotutor is a tabletop physical AI tutor designed for flipped learning classrooms. Based on course materials prepared by the instructor, it asks questions and raises counterarguments during student discussions.

In flipped learning, students study lecture material beforehand and spend class time on active discussion and exercises. While the format depends on active participation, low student engagement during discussions remains a common challenge.

The Tokyo field study investigated whether low participation in flipped learning is similarly prevalent abroad, whether educators are receptive to a physical AI tutor, and what limitations exist in current classroom tools. The team interviewed a professor at the Graduate School of Education, the University of Tokyo, on the causes of low student engagement in higher education, and surveyed and interviewed undergraduate students at the University of Tokyo about the limitations they experience in existing courses.

Cotutor aims to expand its deployment to educational institutions overseas and enter the global market.
