In September and October 2026, SIGMA held ROS2 training sessions, a career session, and a lab tour for new members, and participated in the SNU Creative Design Fair.

From September 7 to October 1, we ran three ROS2 training sessions for new members. ROS2 is a middleware framework widely used in robotics software development.

On September 9 and 10, many members took part in the SNU Creative Design Fair, which SIGMA joins every year.

On September 10, we co-hosted a career session with Robai, and on September 14, members toured Prof. Yongtaek Hong's lab, AXEL (Advanced fleXible Electronics Laboratory).
