On January 24, 2026, SIGMA held its 2025 Year-End Showcase to review the projects and activities completed over the past year.

Throughout 2025, SIGMA ran Arduino training for new members alongside elective courses in AI and Fusion, and participated in the 14th SNU Creative Design Fair. Members also carried out industry collaboration projects with PORTAL301 in industrial robotics and autonomous driving, and conducted field research in Tokyo in December during iREX 2025.
